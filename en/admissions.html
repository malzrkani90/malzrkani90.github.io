Admissions

Admission to the College of Agriculture runs through the Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research's central admission system, within the general (scientific) track, across the College's five specializations.

Central admission requirements

Needed from Registration and Student Affairs: the current admission requirements for each of the five specializations for this academic year (required averages, any special conditions).

Student handbook

Needed: the current student handbook (the latest version available, per the project's reference list, is from 2024).

Student ID cards

Needed: the current student-ID application form, if it has changed from the previous version.

Academic calendar

Needed: the academic calendar for the current year (semester start dates, official holidays, exam periods) — kept in one place here instead of repeating it per department.

Exam schedule

Needed: the final exam schedule for the current semester from Registration and Student Affairs. (The current broken link to this schedule on the live site is replaced by this section once the file is available.)

College of Agriculture — University of Wasit

Founded in 2008, the College prepares specialists in plant and animal production, soil science and food science, and serves the farming community of Wasit Governorate.
