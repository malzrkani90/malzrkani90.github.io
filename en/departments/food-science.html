Department of Food Science

The Department of Food Science covers the processing, preservation and quality evaluation of food products, spanning dairy and meat technology, sugar and date processing, food chemistry and microbiology, additives, and packaging.

This introduction is a working draft based on the department's actual course load (below), not a translation of an official department profile. It will be replaced once the department provides an approved text.
