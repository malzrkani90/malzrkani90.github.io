Department of Plant Protection

The Department of Plant Protection covers the control of insect and non-insect pests, plant and fungal diseases, and weeds, together with pesticide safety and early-detection techniques.

This introduction is a working draft based on the department's actual course load (below), not a translation of an official department profile. It will be replaced once the department provides an approved text.
