Department of Soil Science & Water Resources

The Department of Soil Science and Water Resources covers soil fertility, classification, physics and chemistry, together with irrigation, drainage and land reclamation, and remote sensing for agricultural surveying.

This introduction is a working draft based on the department's actual course load (below), not a translation of an official department profile. It will be replaced once the department provides an approved text.
