College of Agriculture — University of Wasit · Founded 2008

Preparing specialists in crop and livestock production, serving food security in Wasit and Iraq

Five scientific departments, undergraduate and graduate programs, and applied research and agricultural extension connected to the fields and livelihoods of Wasit Governorate.

Departments Admissions

Our threefold mission

As in established colleges of agriculture, our work rests on three integrated pillars.

Teaching

Bachelor's programs in five scientific specializations, and graduate (Master's) study in several of them, with theory and lab curricula updated each semester.

Research

Applied research in crop and animal production and in soil and water health, supervised by faculty with graduate-student participation.

Extension

Carrying research findings to farmers and livestock producers in Wasit through field workshops and direct technical consultation.

The five scientific departments

Each department spans a four-year Bachelor's program, dedicated laboratories, and a teaching staff.

Food Science

Processing, storage and quality evaluation of food products — from dairy and meat to sugar, dates and food additives.

Department page

Soil Science & Water Resources

Soil fertility and classification, irrigation and drainage, land reclamation, and remote sensing and its agricultural applications.

Department page

Medicinal Plants and Health Nutrition

Production of cereal, legume, forage and medicinal/pharmaceutical crops, variety improvement and plant genetics, and modern harvest technology.

Department page

Plant Protection

Control of insect pests, plant diseases and weeds, pesticide safety, and modern techniques for early detection.

Department page

Animal Production

Breeding and improvement of livestock and poultry, their nutrition and health, and efficient dairy and meat production.
